SPECIES 01

Tyrannosaurus Rex

LATE CRETACEOUS
Diet Carnivore
Length Around 12 m
Period Cretaceous
Type Theropod
One of the most famous dinosaurs ever discovered, T. rex had enormous jaws and powerful teeth.
SPECIES 02

Triceratops

LATE CRETACEOUS
Diet Herbivore
Length Around 9 m
Defense Three horns
Type Ceratopsian
Triceratops had a huge skull, a protective frill, and three distinctive horns.
SPECIES 03

Velociraptor

LATE CRETACEOUS
Diet Carnivore
Length Around 2 m
Feature Curved claw
Type Dromaeosaur
Real Velociraptors were much smaller than their movie versions and evidence indicates they had feathers.
SPECIES 04

Spinosaurus

CRETACEOUS
Diet Carnivore
Feature Large sail
Habitat Near water
Type Theropod
Spinosaurus had a long, narrow skull and a distinctive sail-like structure along its back.
SPECIES 05

Stegosaurus

LATE JURASSIC
Diet Herbivore
Length Around 9 m
Defense Tail spikes
Type Thyreophoran
Stegosaurus was famous for the large plates along its back and the spikes on its tail.
SPECIES 06

Brachiosaurus

LATE JURASSIC
Diet Herbivore
Length Around 23 m
Feature Long neck
Type Sauropod
Brachiosaurus had long front legs and a tall neck, allowing it to reach vegetation high above the ground.
SPECIES 07

Ankylosaurus

LATE CRETACEOUS
Diet Herbivore
Length Around 8 m
Defense Armored body
Weapon Tail club
Ankylosaurus was heavily armored and had a powerful club-like tail for defense.
SPECIES 08

Allosaurus

LATE JURASSIC
Diet Carnivore
Length Around 9 m
Feature Large jaws
Type Theropod
Allosaurus was one of the major predators of the Late Jurassic and had powerful jaws and sharp teeth.
SPECIES 09

Diplodocus

LATE JURASSIC
Diet Herbivore
Length Around 25 m
Feature Whip-like tail
Type Sauropod
Diplodocus had an extremely long neck and tail, making it one of the most recognizable sauropods.
